## 🎯 What Is This?

wheres-my-meme is a simple, free tool that helps you find any meme on your Android phone or Windows PC by searching for the text written inside the image. No internet needed. No complicated setup. Just download, run, and search.

## 🤔 Why You Need This

You have hundreds of memes saved on your device. You remember a funny one with the text "Nobody: ..." but you can't find it. Scrolling through thousands of images is frustrating. wheres-my-meme reads the text in your memes and lets you find them instantly by typing a few words.

## 📖 How to Use

### Step 1: Add Your Meme Folder

When you first open wheres-my-meme, you'll see a screen asking you to select a folder.

- On Windows: Click "Choose Folder" and select the folder where you keep your memes.
- On Android: Tap the folder icon and navigate to your meme collection.

### Step 2: Let It Scan

The app will scan all images in that folder and read the text inside them. This might take a few minutes if you have many memes. You'll see progress on the screen.

### Step 3: Search

Once scanning is complete, you'll see a search bar at the top. Type any word or phrase you remember from the meme text. For example, type "nobody" or "distracted boyfriend" or "this is fine."

### Step 4: View Results

Matching memes appear instantly. Click or tap any result to see it full-size. You can also copy, share, or export the image.

## 💡 Tips and Tricks

### 📁 Organize Your Memes First
Put all your memes in one folder for best results. This makes scanning faster and searching easier.

### 🔄 Rescan When You Add New Memes
After adding new memes to your folder, use the "Rescan" button to update the search index.

### 🎯 Use Specific Words
Search for exact words from the meme text. "This is fine" works better than "dog coffee."

### 📊 Filter by Date
You can sort results by date to find older or newer memes.

### 🏷️ Create Collections
Group related memes into collections for even faster access.

## 🆘 Getting Help

### Common Issues

**Problem: App doesn't find any text**
Make sure your images have clear, readable text. Blurry or tiny text might not be recognized.

**Problem: Scan takes too long**
Try organizing memes into smaller folders. Also close other apps to free up memory.

**Problem: App crashes**
Restart your device and try again. If the problem continues, download the latest version.
